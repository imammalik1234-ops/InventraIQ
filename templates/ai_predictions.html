How to read these recommendations
{{ icon('chart',22) }}

1. Model prediction

Linear regression estimates total seven-day demand from daily recorded sales. Missing days count as zero recorded sales; stockouts and lost demand are not captured.

{{ icon('box',22) }}

2. Projected shortfall

Current available stock is compared with model demand. Expired stock is excluded; products are evaluated separately by ID.

max(0, ceil(demand − available stock))
{{ icon('shield',22) }}

3. Business-rule recommendation

A 20% policy buffer is applied. This arithmetic is not an ML prediction. Review supplier lead time, open orders and expiry before purchasing.

max(0, ceil(demand × 1.20) − available stock)

Eligibility: at least 14 calendar days, 7 selling days, activity within 7 days, and chronological holdout MAE at most max(1 unit, holdout daily mean). These checks do not guarantee accuracy and are not confidence scores.
