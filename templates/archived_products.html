Archived products

Archived products retain sales, stock records and audit history, and are unavailable for new operations. Stock must be zero before archiving. Reactivation is not available.

Permanent deletion is an administrator-only API action (DELETE /products/<id>, mode: permanent, current expected_version and CSRF token required). It is allowed only when no inventory, sales, alerts, predictions or audit records exist. A zero-stock inventory record still counts as a dependency.
